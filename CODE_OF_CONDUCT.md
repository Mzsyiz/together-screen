# Code of Conduct

English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or diagnostics.
- Acknowledge mistakes and take responsibility.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, insults or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Discrimination or exclusion based on protected characteristics.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, Issues, Pull Requests and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

For a confidential report, contact the repository maintainers through a private communication channel. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports in good faith and take proportionate action. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
